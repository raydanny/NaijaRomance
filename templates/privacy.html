Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

When you use NaijaRomance, we collect:

  • Account data: username, email address, and password (stored as a secure hash).
  • Profile data: name, date of birth, gender, location (state/city), photos, and any other information you voluntarily add to your profile.
  • Usage data: pages visited, messages sent, likes, winks, and last-seen timestamps.
  • Device data: browser type, IP address, and device type for security and analytics purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the matching and messaging features of the Service.
  • To show your profile to other members (subject to your privacy settings).
  • To send you in-app notifications about likes, messages, and matches.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and violations of our Terms of Service.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Profile information you make public (name, photos, state, interests) is visible to other logged-in members. We may share data with law enforcement when required by Nigerian law.

4. Your Privacy Controls

You can control your privacy in Settings → Privacy:

  • Hide profile: prevents your profile from appearing in Browse and Search results.
  • Block users: prevents specific members from seeing your profile or messaging you.
  • Delete account: permanently removes all your data from our servers.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all personal data, photos, and messages are permanently deleted. Some anonymised aggregate statistics may be retained for analytics.

6. Cookies

NaijaRomance uses a session cookie to keep you logged in. No third-party advertising or tracking cookies are used.

7. Security

Passwords are stored using industry-standard bcrypt hashing. We use CSRF protection on all forms. Photos are stored on our server and served only to authenticated users.

8. Children's Privacy

NaijaRomance is strictly for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we discover a user is under 18, we will terminate their account immediately.

9. Contact

For privacy-related requests or concerns, please contact us.
